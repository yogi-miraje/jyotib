Dubai Police Officers’ Club Lounge

Preconstruction and procurement for the design and build of an officers’ club interior.

Location
Al Wasl, Dubai
Type
Commercial interior
Value
AED 720,000
Duration
2 months
Completed
2018
Delivery
Design and build

My role

Pre-Construction Manager, JBC Design-Build / Joga Ram Group. My work covered estimating, design coordination, finish procurement and subcontractor selection.

Scope

Coordinated the design and procurement of timber slats, marble finishes, glass partitions and integrated lighting, including the transitions between walls and ceilings.

Result

The club interior was delivered over a two-month program in 2018. The gallery shows the design proposal, installation stage and completed lounge and meeting room.
