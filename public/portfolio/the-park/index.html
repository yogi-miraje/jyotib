The Park

Architectural design and coordination for a high-rise residential development in Worli.

Location
Worli, Mumbai
Type
Residential architecture
Size
2,800 homes
Site
17.5 acres
Client
Lodha Group
Design team
Kapadia Associates with WOHA

My role

Project Architect, Kapadia Associates Design LLP. I contributed to design development, construction drawings, finish specifications and consultant coordination as part of the architecture team.

Scope

Developed the residential design with the project team and coordinated architectural information with structural and MEP consultants. The wider master plan combined residential towers and shared landscaped spaces.

Result

The design work established tower forms, planted terraces and resident open spaces. These images are architectural visualizations and plans from the design phase, rather than completion photographs.
