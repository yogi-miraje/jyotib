Altaire at Queen Anne

Construction management for a 114-home affordable housing community in Lower Queen Anne.

Location
118 W Mercer St, Seattle
Type
Affordable multifamily
Size
114 homes
Status
Under construction
Developer
SRM Development
Partner
Urban League of Metropolitan Seattle

My role

Project Manager, SRM Construction. I lead a team of six across cost control, procurement, subcontractor management and schedule.

Scope

The community includes studios and one-, two- and three-bedroom homes, with one level of below-grade parking. I coordinate procurement, project costs, subcontractors and the construction schedule.

Result

The project is under construction and will provide 114 affordable homes in Lower Queen Anne.
