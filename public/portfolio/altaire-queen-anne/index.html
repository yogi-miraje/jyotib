Altaire at Queen Anne

Construction management for a 114-home affordable housing community in Lower Queen Anne.

Location
118 W Mercer St, Seattle
Type
Affordable multifamily
Size
114 homes
Status
Under construction
Scheduled completion
Q4 2027 (July 2026 update)
Developer
SRM Development
Partner
Urban League of Metropolitan Seattle

My role

Project Manager, SRM Construction. I lead a team of six across cost control, procurement, subcontractor management and schedule.

Scope

The seven-story community includes studios and one-, two- and three-bedroom homes, with below-grade parking. Planned amenities include a community room, bike parking, in-unit laundry and a dog run. I coordinate procurement, project costs, subcontractors and the construction schedule.

Result

The project will provide 114 affordable homes in Lower Queen Anne. SRM’s July 2026 update scheduled completion for the fourth quarter of 2027; its October update reported progress on wood framing.
