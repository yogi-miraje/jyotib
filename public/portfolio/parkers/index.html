Parkers Restaurant

Execution of a restaurant interior fit-out at The Dubai Mall, completed in four months.

Location
The Dubai Mall, Dubai
Type
Restaurant interior
Value
AED 2.1 million
Duration
4 months
Completed
2016
Delivery
Interior fit-out execution

My role

Project Manager, Apple Fit Out Interiors. The project scope was execution of the interior fit-out, with design and technical coordination, procurement and site delivery.

Scope

Coordinated specialist finishes, imported lighting, the storefront and back-of-house kitchen. Procurement had to account for custom materials and the mall’s fire-rating requirements.

Result

The restaurant fit-out was completed in 2016 over a four-month program, bringing the dining areas, servery and kitchen into one operating space.
