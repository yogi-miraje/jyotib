Hello, I am Jyoti.

I am a project manager at SRM Construction in Seattle. I lead teams on Altaire Queen Anne, a 114-home affordable housing project, and the 211-unit Astro Hotel conversion. My work includes budgets, procurement, subcontractor management and schedules.

Earlier I worked eight years in Mumbai and Dubai, first as an architect and then managing interior fit-outs for restaurants, clinics and retail.

I hold a master’s in construction management from the University of Washington and a Bachelor of Architecture from the University of Mumbai. I am a LEED AP BD+C, have completed OSHA 30-hour training and the DBIA core courses.
